GhostQuill 1.0.1

A reliability update for the dictation loop.

  • Switching or unplugging your microphone mid-dictation (AirPods connecting, a headset pulled) now stops the recording with a clear "Microphone changed — try again" instead of silently garbling the capture.
  • A transcription that takes too long can now be cancelled right from the menu bar — GhostQuill returns to Ready, and your next dictation starts fresh.
  • Check for updates on demand: Settings → Application → "Check for Updates…".
  • More reliable insertion into browsers and Electron-based apps (Slack, VS Code, and the like): GhostQuill now gives slow targets more time to consume the paste, so they no longer end up with your previous clipboard contents instead of the transcript.
  • Settings now shows the model that will actually be used — including the default when none is explicitly selected — and flags a failed model download right on the summary row.
  • A stuck transcription now recovers within four minutes at worst, instead of blocking dictation for up to twenty.
