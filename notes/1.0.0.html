GhostQuill 1.0.0

The first public release of GhostQuill — push-to-talk voice typing for your Mac.

  • Hold your shortcut, speak, release — the transcript is typed into whatever app you are working in.
  • Transcription runs entirely on your Mac with whisper.cpp. Audio and transcripts never leave the machine.
  • Pick the Whisper model that fits your Mac — from fastest to most accurate — downloaded on demand.
  • The Scratchpad catches your words when no text field is focused, and doubles as a dictation window.
  • Guided onboarding: permissions, model download, and shortcut setup in a couple of minutes.
  • Automatic background updates.

Requires macOS 15 or later, plus the Microphone and Accessibility permissions.
